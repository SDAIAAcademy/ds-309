Every number in the pack is measured and can be rerun. A claim without a number is a plan, not a result.

Full marks, criterion by criterion. Privacy: a linkage test against the public honours list before and after treatment, k with its quasi-identifiers, the utility change, and counts released under a stated budget with their error. Fairness audit: rates by group and at least one intersection with intervals and counts, a criterion chosen for the harm, findings mapped to the national bias guide, one mitigation measured on rows it was not tuned on. Documentation: datasheet and model card filled from logs, with limits stated. Impact assessment: each risk scored on the national framework's scales from evidence, with residual risk, owner and a reviewer for high risks. Reproducibility: a lock file, seeds, and a README that reruns every number; the live recomputation is scored here.

Format of the review

Ten minutes each, questions and the live recomputation included

1 · The release · 3 min

Risk before and after, the treatment, the budget, what remains.

2 · The audit · 3 min

The largest gap with its interval, its cause, the mitigation and its cost.

3 · Live · 4 min

The assessor picks a slice; you recompute one disparity from your repository, then questions.

Peer feedback. After each review, everyone writes one strength, one question and one suggestion.

The timer runs for ten minutes in all. Pick the slice before the review starts and keep it from the presenter: a school type, a region, a field of study, or two of them crossed. A good recomputation states the rate for each group, the interval and the count, and says when the slice is too small to estimate. If the code does not run, ask what they would change; the reproducibility mark is lost, the others are not.
